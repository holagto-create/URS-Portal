📝 Digital FM-RIS-002 Note — v3 (Aligned with Original Form):
✅ Date field INCLUDED — present in the original FM-RIS-002 (top-right of paper form). In the Google Form, add a Date question (type: Date). The Apps Script captures get('Date') and uses it as the official request date; falls back to the submission timestamp if left blank by the client.

✅ Affiliation field REMOVED — not present in the original FM-RIS-002. Affiliation (SLU / Non-SLU) is determined by the ISM Officer by reviewing the client's School field, then manually entered in Column K of the Google Sheets dashboard. If the client is Non-SLU, the ISM Officer also adjusts Total Fee (Col N) — the 60/40 split recalculates automatically via the onEdit() trigger.

The ISM Officer fills Amount of Payment/O.R. No., Consultant/Statistician, and Availability in the Google Sheets dashboard after payment is confirmed at the Finance Office.
